“Welcome”

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad.

I completed my Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur in 2021 under the supervision of Prof. Arobinda Gupta. Prior to my doctoral study, I received my M.Tech. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati and my B.E. degree from Bengal Engineering and Science University, Shibpur (currently known as IIEST, Shibpur).

In addition to my academic career, I have work experience in the software industry. My research interest lies around the design and analysis of algorithms with an emphasis on temporal graphs.

Research Interests

My research interests are in designing efficient centralised and distributed algorithms to address fundamental problems on dynamic computational systems. In particular, we address different fundamental graph problems on graphs with dynamic topology.

Temporal Graphs

Design of efficient algorithms to construct different graph structures on temporal graphs.

Distributed Algorithms

Design of efficient algorithms to address fundamental problems using autonomous mobile robots.

Heterogeneous Information Networks

Design of efficient algorithms to extract information from information ecosystems modelled using HIN.
